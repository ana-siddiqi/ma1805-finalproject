A Constellation of Technology is an interactive web project that explores the idea of memories and nostalgia. This project ties together the relationship that us as individuals have with technology. Almost as if technology is another family member because it has been there throughout every stage of life and has captured every moment. Therefore, people reflect on moments through their devices to recollect and reminisce on those special memories and share them with others. 

I got the idea of this project whilst listening to a song by one of my favourite bands, Coldplay, specifically their song "A Sky Full of Stars" and I wanted to create a concept of a sky full of memories through stars. 

For this project, I included images and videos from my childhood that I always look back on. These moments were captured on a variety of devices, such as different types of phones and camcorders. This project demonstrates how memories are infinite and technology is forever evolving. 

In this project, there are 6 stars placed around the screen and each star opens up either a video or image from one of my childhood memories. The title "A Constellation of Technology" has a warm yellow colour, and a different font to make it stand out. The emoji stars look is inspired by early 2000's media and simple aesthetics and how I imagine an interactive galaxy would look then. Underneath a star there is a unique small poem describing that memory and the device it was captured on. There is also a small line under that says "Click anywhere to close" which lets the users know that if they click anywhere else around the screen, it exist from the memory back onto the home page. Additionally, the first click on the screen plays background music that I recorded myself, it was from a choir perfomance that I did as a child. The music plays in the background of the home page, the second page and the images, however when a video plays the background music stops to allow the users to hear the audio from the videos. Furthermore, I wanted to make each star glow, however this wasn't working as it kept breaking my code, but if I have more time next time I will incorporate that idea.

On the home page, there is a moon in the top right corner, when the user clicks onto the moon and holds their mouse there, the moon acts sort of like a portal as it shows a popup of the second page. The second page is called "Celestial Devices" and it portrays images of the devices and cameras used in the memories. The title "Celestial Devices" floats around the screen to fit with the galaxy vibe but to also give off a zero gravity atmosphere effect. When the user releases their mouse, the pop up disappears and the home page appears. For the second page I orginally wanted to add several images of devices and make them float like planets with a swirling galaxy gif in the background, however I decided not to proceed with this idea as it kept breaking my code and there were too many errors. In addition to this I initially wanted the second page to open up an external link to a galaxy game that I found, however I decided to make the project soley based on technology as the galaxy game didn't relate to my project's idea and purpose.

The images and videos used as the memories are all from my camera roll, as well as the background music. The stars are from my keyboard. However, the galaxy background image, the moon and the devices were all found online.

I carried out research when creating my idea and came across this website. https://stars.chromeexperiments.com 
I absolutely loved the interactivity of this website, and I have always been fascinated by space and the secrets it holds as well as the concept of time in space (e.g lightyears), so I really wanted to incorporate the idea of space to technology and memories and how just like memories, space is neverending, infinite and so beyond us. Also to link back to nostalgia, growing up my father would always refer to me as his star, so this is also another place where the idea was fueled by. I want users to be immersed by this experience of each star holding a memory. 

One slight issue with this project is that when it is first opened as a live server, the stars are quite close together and they overlap one another. However when the screen/website is refreshed, everything goes back to its correct position.
